Everything above is your scroll position. The products fly in, one comes forward and is read region by region, hands over to the other, and they settle back — all of it scrubbed, so scrolling back runs it backwards.

Underneath, the lotus never stops: four whorls of petals counter-rotate and breathe, 56 marks orbit around them, and a sweep crosses the field — whether you are scrolling or not. It is drawn, not photographed, so it is sharp at any size and on any display, carries the product hues exactly, and has no licence attached to it. The two App Store icons are the real ones, straight out of each app's asset catalogue: they sit paired above the flower, and whichever product is being read flies down to stand beside its own caption.
