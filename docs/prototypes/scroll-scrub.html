What this is

01 is svg.createDrawable with its progress bound to scroll instead of to time. 02 and 03 hold your real captures still and move a spotlight through them, one feature at a time, scrubbed. 04 is the other mode — it triggers once on arrival and plays at its own speed. That last one is what the live site does today, which is why it reads as so much less than the three above it.

All anime.js, which already ships in your bundle: marginal cost 0 kB. Both captures are the existing xbill-screen.webp and spade-screen.webp, so the 233 kB image budget is untouched. No 3D engine anywhere.

Reduce Motion is on in your system settings, so every scene is showing its finished state and nothing is scrubbing. That setting is also why the live site shows you no motion.
