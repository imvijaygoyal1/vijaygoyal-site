A prototype, not the site. Six placeholder cards, shaped exactly like a phone screen (768 × 1670): three for xBill, three for The Shady Spade. Each will take a real screen later by setting one line in the code. The deck fans out at the opening, each app's cards come forward one at a time and rest, the six spread into an exploded line drawing on paper for How I build, and they gather into a fan at the end.

The cards follow your scroll rather than snapping to it, settling over about half a second, so a fast flick glides. Scrolling back runs it backwards.
