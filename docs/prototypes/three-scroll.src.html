How I build

Problem to store, part by part.

  1. Discover. Use the thing.
  2. Define. Decide what it is not.
  3. Design. In SwiftUI, on a real device.
  4. Build. Offline-first, secure where money is.
  5. Refine. Tests behind every release.
  6. Ship. Submit, get approved, repeat.

A prototype, not the site. There is no phone: each screen is your real App Store capture, cut into its own parts (cards, rows, chips, player tiles, playing cards). They float apart in depth at the opening and slot back one after another until the screenshot is whole. While you read an app, its parts lift out and drop back in turn. xBill flies apart as The Shady Spade assembles, the parts become an exploded line drawing on paper for How I build, and everything settles at the end.

Everything is your scroll position, so scrolling back runs it backwards. Built with three.js; the dial and ruler follow animejs.com; headlines arrive with anime.js.
